## Full description

**Paopoa StageMic** is a free OBS Studio filter that makes your voice sound produced, with no extra
software, cables or routing. Add it to your mic in OBS and everything happens in one window.

**Sound great in one click**
* Presets for talking, karaoke, pop and ballads, ready to go.
* **Set up my voice**: sing for 11 seconds and it sets the input level, noise gate and de-esser for your mic and room.
* **Simple mode** shows only what matters; **Advanced mode** opens every setting.

**Tune (pitch correction)**
* From gentle and natural to the hard, robotic stepping effect.
* Key and scale, voice type (high / mid / low), speed, formant and pitch shift, with a live pitch graph.

**Fun voices**
Chipmunk, Deep Monster, Robot, Telephone, Megaphone, Bathroom, Concert Hall, Cave Echo and Alien.

**Everything a vocal needs**
Noise gate, auto level, EQ with a live spectrum, harshness and plosive tamers, de-esser, compressor,
multiband, warmth and air, chorus, echo, reverb and a limiter that keeps your stream from clipping.
Power users can re-order the whole chain and save their own presets.

**Light and fast**
* No added delay (Tune adds 20 ms only while it's switched on).
* About 1.5% of one CPU core for the default sound; 0% extra for the window while it's closed.

**Requirements**
Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit), OBS Studio 31 or newer.

**Free and open source** (AGPL-3.0). The source code is a separate download on this page.

## Install, in four steps

1. Close OBS (also from the tray icon near the clock).
2. Run **StageMic-Setup.exe**. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click **More info → Run anyway**.
3. Open OBS → Audio Mixer → three dots next to your mic → **Filters** → **+** → **Paopoa StageMic**.
4. Click **Open Paopoa StageMic**, then **Set up my voice**.

Not showing up in OBS? Open Filters on your **microphone** (it's an audio filter), restart OBS fully,
and check you have OBS 31 or newer.
